Project adaptations: fit the outfit and hair to the adult anatomy; transfer
skin weights; pose onto hands and knees; add an opaque cloth lining; adjust
surface materials; bake skinning into meshes; simplify geometry; resize and
compress embedded textures. v41 additions: extended shirt, rolled fabric hems, cloth wear, one relative
breathing morph and an embedded looping breathing clip. The real-time motion
keeps palms, fingers, knees and feet fixed. No runtime skeleton and no
external asset request.
