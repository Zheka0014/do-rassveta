Credits and licenses of the models, textures, sounds and code used in Before Dawn: The Forest Remembers. The author of the game thanks everyone listed below.

Asset sources

The woman in the basement v41

Anatomy and the base skeleton — Female base mesh; clothing, adult skin textures, eyes, eyebrows, eyelashes, teeth and hair — Jordan. Both assets are published by Innerscene, based on MakeHuman, under CC0. For the game the clothing and hair were fitted to the anatomy, the skin weights were transferred, the figure was posed on her palms and knees, and an opaque cloth layer was added. The pose is baked, the geometry is simplified and the embedded textures are compressed. Full details: ATTRIBUTION.txt. In v41 the shirt was lengthened, and volumetric folds, dirt color, one breathing morph and a looping clip were added.

The Watcher v36

The complete model Zombie Walk Test — OSCAR CREATIVO, CC BY 4.0. The author's geometry, UVs, skinning, color and normal textures are kept. The scale and materials were changed. Idle / Walk / Run animations — Adobe Mixamo, from the Three.js Soldier example; retargeted to the model's skeleton. Added: foot placement on uneven ground, gaze direction and a procedural swing. Detailed attribution: ATTRIBUTION.txt.

Three.js 0.180.0 and GLTFLoader/BufferGeometryUtils — MIT. License text: dist/vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt.

Assets made for the game

The title-screen forest and the flare's flame texture were made with the built-in ImageGen. Flame texture: dist/assets/flame.png; the exact prompt is saved in asset-prompts/flame.txt. The cabin geometry, the volumetric campfire flame, the hand-and-flashlight model, the glove's cloth surface, the rain effects, the procedural sky, clouds and moon, the post-processing, the game logic and the procedural sound were created for this project.

The cemetery, fence, gravestones, coffin, hanged figures, animations, creaking ropes, whispers and the bell were created for the project. Stone and wooden surfaces reuse the Poly Haven materials listed above.

The stretcher, the radio, the bone charms, the stained cloth and their sounds were also created procedurally for the project.

The basement, the stairs and hatch, the Double, the seated figures, traps, tripwires, extra graves and footprints were created for the project; their materials reuse the sources above. The radio uses procedural static, text lines and a local Web Speech API voice in the game's language, if one is installed. Spatial footsteps and door sounds are synthesized with the Web Audio API.
